When I was very young

I was studying in 1st standard. One day, I travelled from Chennai to Thoothukudi by train with my family.

That day, I had a small fight with my parents. I told them that I wanted to sleep on the upper berth. they give a permission , so I climbed up and slept there.

After some time, I woke up and saw many police officers standing near me. They asked me, “What happened? Are you hurt?” I was confused and looked at my mother. Then my mother asked me what happened.

I told nothing.

The police scolded my parents and left.

Later, I came to know what had actually happened. While I was sleeping, I had fallen from the upper berth to the lower. But I was sleeping so deeply that I didn’t even know that I had fallen.

It was really scary for my parents, but I didn’t even know what happened.
