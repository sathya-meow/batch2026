The uncomfortable part

A phone-free future sounds exciting. But should we trust it?

Technology can make life easier, but a connected world also creates new questions.

The final question

Maybe the future isn't a better phone.

Maybe it's a world where we don't need one.

Technology can disappear from our hands without disappearing from our lives. The interesting question is not what replaces the phone — it is what kind of life we build after it.
